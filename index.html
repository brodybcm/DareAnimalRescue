Our Guiding Purpose

The 4 Pillars of D.A.R.E.

Every dollar raised and hour spent is dedicated to fulfilling these core foundational commitments to our community animals.

D

Defending

Giving a voice to the voiceless. We defend mistreated, abandoned, and vulnerable animals in our community against neglect and cruelty.

A

Advocating

Pushing for stronger animal welfare laws, spay/neuter initiatives, humane shelter conditions, and community pet resources throughout Georgia and Florida.

R

Rescuing

Pulling pets from high-kill shelters, securing urgent medical treatment, providing loving foster homes, and carefully matching each pet with forever families.

E

Educating

Empowering our neighbors with education on proper pet care, heartworm prevention in South Georgia, and the urgent necessity of spaying and neutering.

Quick & Simple Guide

How To Adopt From D.A.R.E.

We’ve simplified our adoption process into 4 easy steps designed to ensure a happy, lifelong match for both you and your new pet.

1

Submit Application

Complete our straightforward online application with details about your home, schedule, and living arrangements.

Takes only 3 minutes
2

Vet & Home Check

We verify that any current pets are up to date on core vaccines and spayed/neutered, and confirm pet allowances with landlords or HOAs.

Ensures pet safety
3

Meet & 2-Week Trial

Meet your chosen companion in person, followed by a 2-week foster-to-adopt trial period in your home with zero pressure to make sure it's the right fit.

Zero pressure trial
4

Welcome Home ($100)

Finalize the adoption contract! Our flat $100 adoption donation covers complete vetting, vaccinations, surgery, testing, and microchip.

Official adoption day

Golden Years Mission

The D.A.R.E. Senior Sanctuary

Lifelong love, dignity, and specialized veterinary care for our permanent senior and unadoptable residents who call D.A.R.E. their forever home.

A Promise of Lifelong Dignity & Compassion

When animals reach advanced age, endure chronic medical conditions, or have special behavioral needs that make standard adoption impractical, D.A.R.E. makes an unbreakable commitment: they will never be abandoned or forgotten.

Our permanent senior residents spend their golden years surrounded by soft beds, daily companionship, nutritious diets, and specialized veterinary care—including arthritis medications, thyroid therapies, and regular checkups.

Permanent Home

Guaranteed lifelong shelter and unconditional affection.

Senior Medicine

Joint supplements, heart medications, and pain relief.

Peaceful Comfort

Gentle routines, soft bedding, and loving care every day.
