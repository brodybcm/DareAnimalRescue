Our Mission

Since our inception in 2014, DARE's mission has been to Defend animals who can not defend themselves, Advocate for animals who can not speak for themselves, Rescue animals in need, and Educate pet owners, as well as the community about issues that affect animals, with an emphasis on proper pet care & spay/neuter.

With 40+ combined years of rescue experience, the time has come to scale back operations, changing our mission somewhat. Our focus has shifted to finding loving homes for our final adoptable pets and providing comfortable, life-long living situations for our permanent seniors and unadoptable pets.

[We are not an Animal Shelter]

Found a Stray Animal?

Step-by-Step Community Action Guide

Notice Regarding Stray Intake: D.A.R.E. Animal Rescue is closed to new intake as we focus on our remaining adoptables and senior sanctuary pets. Following these essential steps gives the pet the highest chance of safely reuniting with their family:

1. Scan for a Microchip (100% Free)

Bring the stray to any veterinary clinic or animal hospital in Lowndes County. They will scan for a registered microchip free of charge with no appointment required.

2. Contact Lowndes County Animal Services

Call (229) 671-2760 or visit 337 Gil Harbin Industrial Blvd, Valdosta, GA. Municipal animal services is the legal central registry where searching pet owners look first.

3. Post on Local Social Media

Post clear photos, the exact location found (cross streets), and contact details to Facebook groups like "Lost and Found Pets of Lowndes County / Valdosta GA", PawBoost, and Nextdoor.

4. Provide Safe Temporary Shelter

Keep the animal secured in a quiet, shaded space with fresh water. Avoid feeding rich or unfamiliar food right away.

Call Lowndes Animal Services (229-671-2760)

Need Help Rehoming Your Pet?

Safe, Compassionate Placement Options

Notice Regarding Surrenders: D.A.R.E. Animal Rescue is closed to owner surrenders. To ensure your beloved companion goes to a safe, thoroughly screened home, please review these vital guidelines:

Never Post Pets for Free Online

Never advertise pets as "free to good home" or post on Craigslist/Facebook Marketplace. Free animals are frequently targeted for dog-fighting bait, puppy mills, and fraudulent resale.

1. Use Safe Peer-to-Peer Rehoming Platforms

These trusted platforms allow you to keep your pet in your loving home while safely screening adopters:

  • Adopt-a-Pet Rehome: Visit rehome.adoptapet.com to create a profile and review applications securely.
  • Get Your Pet: Visit getyourpet.com for direct adopter-to-guardian transfers.

2. Complete All Veterinary Care Before Rehoming

Ensure your pet is spayed/neutered, current on rabies and core vaccines, and on heartworm prevention. Fully vetted pets find safe homes much faster.

3. Lowndes County Animal Services

If you are facing an emergency hardship, contact Lowndes County Animal Services at (229) 671-2760 regarding official surrender intake policies.

Call Lowndes Animal Services (229-671-2760)

Lost & Found Pets / Wildlife

Lowndes County Recovery & Georgia Wildlife Resources

Notice: D.A.R.E. does not have a lost & found coordinator on staff. The resources below provide the fastest assistance for regional pet recovery and native wildlife emergencies:

Lost or Found Domestic Pets

  • Visit Lowndes County Animal Services in person: Located at 337 Gil Harbin Industrial Blvd, Valdosta. In-person checks are crucial because breed and coat descriptions often vary on intake forms. Call 229-671-2760.
  • Update Microchip Information: Immediately contact your pet's microchip registry (HomeAgain, 24PetWatch, AKC Reunite) to verify your current phone number and address.
  • Post on Social Media & PawBoost: Post clear photos, location details, and your phone number to local Lowndes County lost & found pet groups and PawBoost.
  • Distribute Physical Flyers: Post brightly colored flyers with clear photos within a 2-mile radius of where your pet was last seen.

Injured or Orphaned Wildlife

Under Georgia state law, domestic rescue organizations cannot legally take in or treat native wildlife (fawns, raccoons, bats, birds of prey, opossums, turtles).

  • Contact a Licensed GA Wildlife Rehabilitator: Visit the Georgia Department of Natural Resources (DNR) Wildlife Resources Division directory at georgiawildlife.com.
  • Safe Containment: Keep the animal in a warm, dark, ventilated cardboard box in a quiet room. Do not attempt to give water or food without rehabber instruction.
Call Lowndes Animal Services (229-671-2760)

Report Animal Cruelty & Neglect

Be Their Voice — Who to Call in Valdosta & Lowndes County

If you witness animal cruelty or neglect, please report it. Animals cannot speak or dial for help — your vigilance saves lives. In Georgia, animal cruelty and intentional neglect are violations of state law.

Within Lowndes County (Outside Valdosta City Limits):

Contact Lowndes County Animal Services or the Sheriff's Office:

• Lowndes County Animal Services: (229) 671-2760
• Lowndes County Sheriff's Office: (229) 671-2900 (or 911 for emergency in progress)

Within Valdosta City Limits:

Contact Valdosta Police Department non-emergency dispatch or dial 911:

• Valdosta Police Department: (229) 242-2606
• Emergency in Progress: Dial 911 immediately

Key Details to Report:

  • Exact street address and location landmarks.
  • Date, time, and specific conditions observed (no water, no shade/shelter, severe tethering, open wounds, extreme emaciation).
  • Safe photo or video documentation if possible without trespassing.
Call Lowndes County (229-671-2760) Call Valdosta PD (229-242-2606)

Adoption Eligibility Requirements

Before You Apply — Please Review Our 9 Adoption Requirements

1

Age Guidelines

Adopter, Children & Senior Matching
  • • Primary Adopter: Must be at least 25 years old (exceptions considered case-by-case for well-qualified applicants 21+).
  • • "6 Under 6" Rule: We do not place puppies under 6 months old or dogs under 6 lbs into homes with children under 6 years old.
  • • Toy & Small Breeds: Chihuahuas, Yorkies, Shih Tzus, Dachshunds, etc. require children in the home to be 8–10+ years old depending on the dog.
  • • Cats & Kittens: Children in the home must be 6+ years old for kittens; adult cats are matched by temperament.
  • • Senior Adopters: We pair senior adopters with calm, mature adult or senior companions rather than young puppies/kittens.
"We do Age-Appropriate Matching between pets & people"
2

Vet Checks

15-Year Pet Care History

We verify veterinary history going back 15 years for all current and past pets to confirm consistent standard of care:

  • • Spay / Neuter: All current/past pets must be spayed or neutered (unless medically exempt by a veterinarian).
  • • Core Vaccines: Current on Rabies and annual DHPP / FVRCP boosters.
  • • Continuous Heartworm Prevention: Year-round prescription heartworm prevention history is mandatory for all dogs in the Deep South.
Call your vet ahead to authorize release of records to D.A.R.E.
3

Housing & Environment

Ownership & Landlord Checks
  • • Homeowners: Ownership is verified via county property/tax records.
  • • Renters: Written or direct verbal landlord verification is required confirming pet permission, breed/weight limits, and pet deposit compliance.
  • • Safe Living Space: A home visit (in-person or virtual video walkthrough) is required prior to placement.
Stable, pet-approved housing required
4

Fencing & Indoor Living

Family Companion Standard
  • • Strictly Indoors: All D.A.R.E. dogs and cats must live indoors as cherished family members — never kept outside on chains, tethers, kennels, or porches.
  • • Cats: Must be 100% indoor-only to protect them from traffic, predators, and disease.
  • • Fencing for Dogs: Most dogs require a secure, physically fenced yard. Invisible/shock fences are not accepted as primary containment.
100% indoor family companions
5

1st Time Pet Owners

Case-by-Case Matching

First-time pet owners are welcomed and evaluated on a case-by-case basis! We require personal references, a pre-selected local veterinarian, and openness to guidance so we can match you with an easygoing, beginner-friendly pet.

Personal references & chosen vet clinic required
6

Stable Income Source

Financial Capability

Applicants must have a verifiable, stable source of income (employment, retirement, disability, etc.) to afford quality food, monthly heartworm/flea prevention, annual wellness exams, and unexpected veterinary emergencies. Employer name and contact info are requested on the application.

Prepared for routine & emergency veterinary care
7

Time Commitment

Daily Quality Attention

Rescue pets need daily companionship, exercise, potty breaks, and training. Dogs should not be crated or left alone for excessive hours every day without a midday break, doggy daycare, or family member present.

A 10–15+ year lifetime commitment
8

Sole Adult / Single Adopter

Contingency Care & Relocation Policy

If you are the sole adult in your household, we require a designated local, permanent Secondary Caregiver (family member or close friend) who is screened and signs a Secondary Caregiver Agreement to step in if an emergency, hospitalization, or unexpected life event occurs.

Screened Secondary Caregiver Agreement required
9

Military Personnel

Moody AFB & Beyond

We deeply appreciate our military service members! Because deployments, TDYs, and PCS orders can happen unexpectedly, active-duty military applicants follow our Secondary Caregiver Policy (Requirement #8) so your pet always has a pre-approved, loving safety net during deployments or transitions.

Thank you for your service — we help ensure a seamless plan for your pet!
